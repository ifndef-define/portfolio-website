[01 // ARCHITECTURE BRIEF]

About

Engineering at the boundary of hardware, firmware, and computation.

I'm drawn to problems that require thinking across abstraction layers—from PCB layout to firmware architecture to real-time control algorithms. I believe the best engineers understand the full stack: how power flows, how signals propagate, how mechanical systems respond, and how code maps to silicon.

My focus areas: embedded systems, PCB & FPGA design, robotics & control, and the communication interfaces that tie them together. I build prototypes, measure behavior, debug at multiple abstraction levels, and iterate toward reliable systems.

B.S. Computer Engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (graduating May 2027).
